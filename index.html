What SAT is

SAT (Smart Ass Trading) is a WAX token (SAT@sattoken1111), not Bitcoin satoshis. It is tied to the LEEF Trader platform and its Telegram bot. Bot subscriptions paid in Telegram Stars, USDT or WAX are split by the smartatrader contract, and – (live from the splits table) goes to deepening SAT and LEEF liquidity on Alcor.

Planned allocation of the 100M cap

20,000,000 SAT is issued today (20% of the 100,000,000 max). The 10% community slice has been minted and the first airdrop was sent on 9 Nov 2026. The bars below are still a plan for the full cap; the remaining 80,000,000 SAT is unissued, and the issuer multisig decides when (and whether) it is minted. There is no public sale and minting can never exceed the cap. These contracts are not audited.
