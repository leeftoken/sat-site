What SAT is

SAT (Smart Ass Trading) is a WAX token tied to the LEEF Trader platform and its Telegram bot. Bot subscriptions paid in Telegram Stars, USDT or WAX are split by the smartatrader contract, and – (live from the splits table) goes to deepening SAT and LEEF liquidity on Alcor.
